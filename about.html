About KeePics.

KeePics is a small business created out of aesthetic choice, creativity, and student entrepreneurship. Founded by a dedicated group of Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMSS-3) students from Citi Global College (CGC) Santa Rosa, our brand was built on a simple mission: to make memory-keeping both exceptionally affordable and deeply personal.

We specialize in crafting premium, fully customizable keychains designed to add an authentic touch to your everyday essentials. Whether it is for your bags, wallets, keys, or a keepsake gift for someone special, our products serve as a portable canvas for your memories, custom style, and identity.

History and Background

What started as an academic venture for our Entrepreneurship curriculum inspired us to rethink standard keychains. We recognized that keychains are central to daily aesthetics—ideally accenting bags and wallets. By integrating custom photo inserts and optional sticker configurations, we designed a way for individuals to express their unique experiences beautifully.

KeePics operates as a localized, self-supported campus project. Due to requirements assigned by our teacher, distribution is safely limited to our peers across the CGC Santa Rosa campus, close friends, and family networks. Thank you for making our development project a true student milestone!
